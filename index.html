ABOUT THE SCHOOL

Learn, connect, and explore new ideas.

The Spring School brings together PhD students, researchers, early-career academics, and industry practitioners for an intensive three days of learning, discussion, and collaboration.

The programme combines lectures, interactive sessions, practical exercises, and opportunities for participants to discuss their own research and projects.

01

Lectures

Learn from leading researchers and practitioners around the world.

02

Hands-on sessions

Apply concepts through practical exercises and projects.

03

Networking

Meet researchers and participants from different institutions in the world.

TOPICS

What we will explore

The Spring School covers a broad range of topics related to modern AI and software engineering.

01

Recap of Software Engineering

Software Engineering principles and best practices, Requirements engineering, Software testing/quality assurance, DevOps practices and system observability

02

Software Architecture, Design and Modeling

Software architecture fundamentals and stakeholder perspectives Domain analysis and system decomposition Modeling techniques including UML, architectural views, patterns, and styles

03

AI and LLM for Software Engineering

Machine learning techniques for anomaly detection in software systems Predictive analytics for software quality and performance Applications of Large Language Models in software engineering tasks Prompt engineering for software analytics and automation
